Install Dock Companion

It takes a minute. Your Mac asks for one confirmation the first time, because the app comes straight from this site instead of the App Store.

Steps

  1. Download and unzipDouble-click DockCompanion.zip.
  2. Move it to ApplicationsDrag Dock Companion into your Applications folder.
  3. Open it oncemacOS says it can't verify the developer. That's expected. Click Done.
  4. Allow itOpen System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  5. Send your characterCome back here and click Send it to my Dock. Your character jumps right onto your Dock.

No "Open Anyway" button? Files received through messaging apps like WhatsApp get an extra lock from macOS. Open Terminal (Applications → Utilities), paste this and press Return:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Dock Companion.app"

It only removes the "downloaded from the internet" flag from this app. Downloading straight from your browser skips this step.

Questions

Is it safe?

Yes. The app only draws your character in a transparent window above the Dock. It doesn't read your files, doesn't go online and doesn't collect anything.

Why does my Mac show a warning?

macOS warns about any app distributed outside the App Store without Apple's paid developer signature. You only confirm it once.

Does it drain my battery?

Barely. When your character stands still, the app uses 0% CPU. While it walks, it stays around 1–2%.

Will it get in the way of my Dock?

No. Clicks go straight through to the Dock. It only reacts when you click the character itself.

Does it work with a side or hidden Dock?

Yes. On a side Dock it walks along the wall. With auto-hide on, it walks along the edge of the screen and hops up when the Dock appears.

Why does it turn around before the end of the Dock?

Without permission, the app estimates your Dock's size and keeps a small margin. From the menu bar, turn on "Use exact Dock position" and allow the app under Accessibility.

How do I open it every time I start my Mac?

Click your character's head in the menu bar and turn on "Open at login".

How do I switch characters?

Make a new one here and click Install on Mac again. It replaces the previous one.

How do I quit or uninstall?

Quit from the menu bar icon. To uninstall, drag the app from Applications to the Trash.
